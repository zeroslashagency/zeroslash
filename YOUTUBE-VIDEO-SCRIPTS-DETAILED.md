## VIDEO 2: "5 Design Mistakes Killing Indian Startup Conversions"

**Length:** 10-12 minutes  
**Target Views:** 100k-300k  
**Filming Date:** Week 2

---

### FULL SCRIPT

#### HOOK (0:00-0:15)

**YOU:**
"I analyzed 50 Indian startup websites. Five mistakes show up EVERYWHERE. And they're costing millions in lost conversions. Let's fix them."

*[Quick montage: 5 websites with red X marks]*

---

#### INTRO (0:15-0:45)

**YOU:**
"Over the past month, I looked at 50 Indian startup websites. SaaS companies, e-commerce brands, fintech apps.

And I found a pattern. The same five mistakes keep appearing. And every single one kills conversions.

So in this video, I'm going to show you these mistakes, why they matter, and exactly how to fix them.

Let's start with the biggest one."

---

#### MISTAKE #1: UNCLEAR VALUE PROPOSITION (0:45-3:00)

**YOU:**
"Mistake number one: Unclear value proposition.

Look at this example..."

*[Show real startup website - anonymized or with permission]*

**YOU (Voiceover):**
"Headline: 'Transform Your Business with AI'

Okay... but WHAT does this product actually do? I have no idea.

Compare that to this:"

*[Show competitor with clear value prop]*

**YOU (Voiceover):**
"Headline: 'Turn customer calls into actionable insights with AI'

See the difference? I immediately know:
- What it does (analyzes calls)
- What I get (actionable insights)
- How it works (AI)

The first one is vague. The second is specific."

**YOU (Back to camera):**
"The rule: Your homepage headline should pass the 5-second test. Can someone understand what you do in 5 seconds? If not, rewrite it.

Bad: 'Next-gen platform for modern businesses'
Good: 'All-in-one CRM for Indian SMBs - ₹999/month'

Specificity wins."

**VISUAL:** Before/after examples from 3 different startups

---

#### MISTAKE #2: TOO MANY CTAs (3:00-5:00)

**YOU:**
"Mistake two: Too many calls-to-action. Watch this."

*[Screen recording: Startup website with multiple CTAs]*

**YOU (Voiceover):**
"I land on the homepage. And I see:
- 'Get Started' button
- 'Book a Demo' button
- 'Watch Video' button
- 'Read Case Study' link
- 'Contact Sales' in the nav
- 'Download Whitepaper' popup

SIX different calls-to-action on ONE page.

Which one should I click? I don't know. So I click... none of them."

*[Show heatmap data]*

**TEXT ON SCREEN:**
"Multiple CTAs = Decision paralysis"
"1 primary CTA = 3x more conversions"

**YOU (Back to camera):**
"The psychology is simple: Too many choices = no choice.

The fix: ONE primary CTA per page. Everything else is secondary.

Homepage should have: 'Start Free Trial' (primary) and maybe 'Watch Demo' (secondary, less prominent).

That's it. Don't give users 6 paths. Give them one clear next step."

**VISUAL:** Before (6 CTAs) vs After (1 primary CTA)

---

#### MISTAKE #3: SLOW LOAD TIMES (5:00-7:00)

**YOU:**
"Mistake three: Slow page load. And this is HUGE in India where internet speeds vary."

*[Show loading spinner]*

**YOU (Voiceover):**
"I tested these 50 websites on a 3G connection - because that's still reality for millions of Indians.

Average load time: 8.4 seconds.

And here's what that means:"

*[Show conversion funnel]*

**TEXT ON SCREEN:**
"1 second delay = 7% fewer conversions"
"3 seconds delay = 40% bounce rate"
"8 seconds delay = 80%+ bounce rate"

**YOU (Back to camera):**
"You're losing 80% of visitors before they even SEE your site.

Common culprits:
- Unoptimized images (2-5 MB hero images)
- Too many JavaScript libraries
- No compression
- No CDN

The fix: Use tools like:
- TinyPNG for image compression
- Cloudflare for CDN
- Lighthouse for performance audits

Target: Under 2 seconds on 3G. It's possible. We do it for all our clients."

**VISUAL:** Side-by-side: Slow site (8s) vs Fast site (1.5s)

---

#### MISTAKE #4: MOBILE-UNFRIENDLY (7:00-9:00)

**YOU:**
"Mistake four: Not mobile-friendly. And again, this is critical for India."

*[Show mobile website experience]*

**YOU (Voiceover):**
"80% of Indian internet users are mobile-only. But look at this website on mobile:"

*[Show bad mobile experience]*

- Text too small to read
- Buttons too small to tap  
- Images overflow
- Forms require zooming

"This is from 2023, not 2008. But somehow startups are still shipping desktop-only designs."

**YOU (Back to camera):**
"The fix: Mobile-first design. Design for mobile FIRST, then adapt to desktop.

Key checks:
- Touch targets: Minimum 44x44 pixels
- Text size: Minimum 16px
- Forms: Large input fields
- Images: Responsive, optimized

Test on a real device, not just Chrome DevTools. Borrow your mom's Android phone with 4GB RAM. If it works there, it works anywhere."

**VISUAL:** Mobile-unfriendly vs Mobile-first design

---

#### MISTAKE #5: NO SOCIAL PROOF (9:00-11:00)

**YOU:**
"Mistake five: No social proof. And this is the easiest fix."

*[Show website with no testimonials]*

**YOU (Voiceover):**
"I'm on this SaaS startup's website. They're asking me to pay ₹2,000/month.

But I see:
- No customer testimonials
- No case studies
- No logo bar
- No reviews
- No usage stats

Why should I trust them?"

*[Show competitor with social proof]*

**YOU (Voiceover):**
"Now look at this competitor:
- '10,000+ businesses trust us'
- Customer testimonials with photos
- Case study: '300% revenue increase'
- Logo bar: HDFC, Tata, Reliance
- '4.9/5 stars on G2'

Which one would YOU trust?"

**YOU (Back to camera):**
"The fix: Add social proof everywhere. Even if you're early-stage:

- Got 10 customers? Show testimonials
- Got metrics? '50+ projects delivered'
- Got press? 'Featured in YourStory'
- Got nothing? Use your founder story

People buy from people they trust. Give them reasons to trust you."

**VISUAL:** Checklist of social proof elements

---

#### CONCLUSION (11:00-12:00)

**YOU:**
"So those are the five mistakes:

1. Unclear value proposition
2. Too many CTAs
3. Slow load times
4. Not mobile-friendly
5. No social proof

If your website has even ONE of these, you're leaving money on the table.

Good news: They're all fixable. You don't need a complete redesign. Just focused improvements.

We built a free calculator that shows you exactly what these fixes cost. Link in description.

And if you want us to audit YOUR website for these mistakes, drop your URL in the comments. I'll review as many as I can.

Thanks for watching. See you next week with another redesign."
