## TROUBLESHOOTING

**If upvotes are slow:**
- Double-check all communities posted
- Send personal DMs to close contacts
- Offer to answer questions in comments
- Post in more Facebook groups
- Reach out to design influencers

**If negative comments:**
- Stay calm and professional

- Explain your reasoning
- Offer to improve
- Don't argue or get defensive

**If hunter disappears:**
- Be ready to self-hunt
- Or find backup hunter quickly
- Have 2-3 hunters lined up as backup

**If link breaks:**
- Have staging environment ready
- Monitor uptime with tool
- Have backup hosting plan
- Respond immediately if down
